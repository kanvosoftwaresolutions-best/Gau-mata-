Frequently Asked Questions

Are all your sweets made in pure Desi Ghee?
Yes, absolutely! We strictly use 100% pure desi ghee sourced from special gaushalas for all our traditional sweets.
Do you deliver out of city?
Yes, we take large orders and can arrange secure delivery for weddings and bulk corporate gifting out of the city boundaries.
How long do the sweets stay fresh?
Since we don't use artificial preservatives, most milk-based sweets last for 3-5 days under refrigeration, while dry items like Ladoo and Peda last up to 15-20 days.
